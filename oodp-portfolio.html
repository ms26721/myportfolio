Advanced Object-Oriented Design & Programming

Capstone Project: End of Module Reflective Portfolio
featuring SmartDesk, an AI-assisted IT service-desk platform

Introduction

This portfolio reflects on my learning from the Advanced Object-Oriented Design and Programming module. It is structured around four tasks: demonstrating how I achieved the learning outcomes, showcasing key artefacts with critical commentary, reflecting on academic and employability skills, and presenting a Professional Development Plan. I draw on weekly coding exercises and my Capstone Project, SmartDesk—an AI-assisted IT service-desk platform that classifies support tickets, estimates service costs and generates operational reports. I chose this domain because its security-sensitive data, interchangeable AI components and evolving reporting requirements provide a realistic context for applying the module’s concepts.

Conclusion

This module moved me from knowing patterns and principles to applying them with judgement. SmartDesk demonstrated how secure, layered, pattern-based design and disciplined testing produce systems that are robust today and ready for AI tomorrow, while my reflections on trade-offs and ethics reminded me that good design is ultimately about responsible decisions, not just elegant code.
